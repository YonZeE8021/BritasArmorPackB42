[h1][b]Brita's Armor Pack B42.20 - Standalone Armor Port & Multilingual Fix[/b][/h1]

[b]ARMOR AND CLOTHING ONLY — NO WEAPONS[/b]
[b]11 languages | Project Zomboid Build 42.20 stable[/b]

This is an [b]unofficial, standalone Build 42.20 compatibility port of Brita's Armor Pack[/b]. It contains the clothing, armor, helmets, masks, footwear, backpacks, chest rigs, belts, wearable equipment and night-vision devices from the Armor Pack.

[b]Brita's Weapon Pack is not included.[/b] This mod does not add or spawn Brita firearms, Weapon Pack ammunition, weapon magazines, GunFighter systems or weapon-only attachments. Any Brita firearm found in your game comes from another mod.

[h2]v1.4.5 Multilingual Update[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Full support for 11 languages:[/b] English, Simplified Chinese, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Turkish.
[*]The nine newly supported languages include localized item names, clothing-style actions, wear-position menus, night-vision prompts and options, battery messages and sandbox descriptions.
[*]English and Simplified Chinese remain fully supported.
[/list]

[h2]Main Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Ports Brita's Armor Pack clothing and equipment to Build 42.20.
[*]Repairs the B42 clothing layers of [b]17 ballistic vests, plate carriers and heavy armor pieces[/b], allowing ordinary jackets to remain wearable where appropriate.
[*]Repairs [b]101 backpacks, chest rigs, belts, thigh pouches and other wearable containers[/b], restoring valid equipment slots and storage.
[*]Repairs [b]7 shell bandoliers[/b] as wearable containers with shotgun-shell filtering and the vanilla-style [b]15% reload-speed bonus[/b].
[*]Fixes conflicts involving thigh, chest, accessory and legacy clothing locations.
[*]Fixes the Field Canvas Raincoat and related outerwear layering.
[*]Restores functional batteries for night-vision devices.
[*]Provides Necroa compatibility where applicable.
[/list]

[b]Balance note:[/b] this project focuses on compatibility and functional repairs. It is [b]not[/b] a complete rebalance of every backpack capacity, weight reduction value or equipment statistic.

[h2]Night-Vision System[/h2]

Night-vision devices require an installed, charged standard battery. At the default 1.0x drain rate, one full battery lasts about [b]12 in-game hours[/b]. Low charge causes visible flicker; an empty battery shuts the device down.

The required features of Beathoven's [b]Night Vision Framework[/b] are integrated directly into this port.

[b]Do not enable the standalone NVFramework mod at the same time.[/b]

Original Night Vision Framework author: [b]Beathoven[/b]. Attribution and license information are included in:

[code]NVFramework_ATTRIBUTION.txt[/code]

[h2]Night-Vision Controls[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Keep the night-vision item in the character's main inventory and unequipped.
[*]Right-click the device and insert a charged standard battery.
[*]Wear the Active / ON version, then press [b]V[/b] to toggle night vision.
[*]Unequip the device before removing or replacing its battery.
[/olist]

[h2]Installation and Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Project Zomboid Build 42.20 stable is required.[/b]
[*][b]Do not enable[/b] Brita_2, standalone NVFramework, BritasArmorPackB42Fix or another conflicting Brita Armor port with this mod.
[*]After installing or updating, completely exit and restart Project Zomboid.
